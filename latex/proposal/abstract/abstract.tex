\section*{Abstract}

Facial expression recognition (FER) systems have exhibited a significant boost in accuracy due to recent advancements in deep learning methods. 
Neural networks used in FER studies achieve impressive results, even when trained with challenging in-the-wild (ITW) datasets. 
However, top-performing methods, such as Vision Transformers, come with a heavy resource cost that poses a barrier to real-world use. 
This is particularly problematic for mobile and edge devices, which have limited resources and require high computational efficiency. 
This thesis proposal presents a study aimed at enhancing the accuracy of the lightweight EfficientFace network by Zhao et al. \cite{eff-face2021}.
We plan to leverage a FER architecture based on the Vision Transformer, APViT \cite{apvit2023}, along with local-global feature extraction modules, to improve EfficientFace’s performance without increasing its resource consumption. 
Given the results of the EfficientFace study \cite{eff-face2021} and APViT’s high performance in ITW scenarios, we anticipate that our approach will significantly improve the network’s classification accuracy on the AffectNet dataset.
